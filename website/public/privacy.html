Dustonic desktop app

Dustonic scans and cleans files on your computer. The desktop app does not send scan results, file names, or personal files to Dustonic. License activation sends the license key to our validation endpoint so we can verify its status.

Website

We keep only the information you choose to provide, such as an email sent through a contact form or a purchase needed to issue a license. We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles.

Payments and licenses

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments. License records may include your email, license key, tier, and payment reference so we can provide support and prevent abuse.
